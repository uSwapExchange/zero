## Content Templates

### Comparison Page Template (`/compare/{competitor}`)
1. H1: "uSwap Zero vs {Competitor}: Fee & Feature Comparison"
2. Summary table: fees, tracking, open source, supported tokens, speed
3. Fee comparison with verifiable data
4. Feature-by-feature breakdown
5. Why users switch to uSwap Zero
6. FAQ section (3–5 questions with FAQ schema)
7. CTA: Try a zero-fee swap

### Token Pair Page Template (`/swap/{from}-to-{to}`)
1. H1: "Swap {FROM} to {TO} — Zero Fees"
2. Live quote widget (server-rendered)
3. Supported networks for each token
4. Fee breakdown showing $0 markup
5. Estimated swap time
6. Mini FAQ (2–3 questions)
7. CTA: Start swap button (links to homepage with pre-filled pair)

### Educational Article Template (`/learn/{slug}`)
1. H1: Descriptive, keyword-rich title
2. TL;DR summary (2–3 sentences — good for AI citation)
3. Body content with subheadings (H2/H3)
4. Internal links to relevant product pages
5. Sources and references (builds E-E-A-T)
