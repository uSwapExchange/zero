## What is uSwap Zero?

uSwap Zero is a zero-fee cross-chain crypto swap tool. It lets you swap one cryptocurrency for another across different blockchains without paying any fees, creating an account, or providing personal information.

You can use it through the web interface or the Telegram bot. Your funds are never held by uSwap — they go directly through the swap, making it fully non-custodial. There is no sign-up, no KYC, and no tracking.

The web interface does not use JavaScript, which means there is nothing running in your browser that could track you or be exploited. The entire project is open source, so anyone can verify exactly how it works.

## What can uSwap Zero do?

uSwap Zero lets you swap crypto across 29+ blockchains, including Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, and many more. Over 140 tokens are supported.

You can use it through the web interface or the Telegram bot. Both give you real-time order tracking so you always know the status of your swap. QR codes are provided for easy mobile deposits — just scan and send.

The Telegram bot also supports inline queries, so you can generate swap links directly inside any Telegram chat without opening the bot separately.

## How do I get started?

No account is needed. You can start swapping right away.

**On the web:** Visit the site, pick the tokens you want to swap, enter your receiving address and refund address, get a quote, and send your deposit to the provided address.

**On Telegram:** Message the bot with /start and use the buttons to choose your tokens, enter your addresses, and configure your swap. The bot walks you through each step.

## Is uSwap Zero free?

Yes. uSwap Zero charges absolutely zero fees. There is no markup on exchange rates and no hidden charges of any kind.

The only cost you will see is the market maker spread, which is the small difference between the spot price and the rate you are offered. This is not a fee that uSwap charges — it is the natural cost of the swap being fulfilled by a market maker on the other side of the trade. Every exchange has this spread, but uSwap adds nothing on top of it.

## Is uSwap Zero safe?

uSwap Zero is designed with safety and transparency in mind.

It is non-custodial, meaning your funds go directly to the swap network. uSwap never holds your crypto at any point during the process.

The project is fully open source, so anyone can audit the code and verify there is nothing hidden. The Verify page on the site lets you check the exact commit hash, build time, and link to the source code for the version you are using.

The web interface uses no JavaScript, which eliminates an entire category of client-side tracking and security risks.

When you create a swap, you provide a refund address. If anything goes wrong and the swap cannot be completed, your funds are returned to that address.

## How does uSwap Zero make money?

It doesn't. uSwap Zero is a zero-fee, open-source project. There are no hidden fees, no markup on rates, and no referral commissions taken from users. What you see in the quote is exactly what the market maker offers, with nothing added on top.
