# uSwap Zero — SEO Strategy

## Executive Summary

uSwap Zero is a zero-fee, zero-tracking, open-source crypto swap frontend serving 140+ tokens across 29+ blockchains. The product's strongest differentiators — zero fees, no JavaScript tracking, open-source verifiability — are also its strongest SEO angles. The current site has **no SEO foundation** (no meta descriptions, no OG tags, no robots.txt, no sitemap, no structured data), which means every improvement will have outsized impact.

**Primary goal:** Capture organic traffic from crypto traders searching for fee-free, private, and trustworthy swap services.

## Competitive Positioning

uSwap Zero occupies a unique niche: **the only open-source, zero-fee, zero-tracking crypto swap frontend**. Most competitors (ChangeNow, SimpleSwap, StealthEX, Exolix) charge 0.25–4% markup and use extensive JavaScript tracking. The existing case study proving competitor markups on NEAR Intents is a powerful, data-backed content asset.

**Positioning statement for SEO:** "The only crypto swap that charges nothing, tracks nothing, and hides nothing."

- [ ] Structure comparison data in parseable tables
- [ ] Add FAQ schema to how-it-works and case study pages
- [ ] Publish clear, quotable claims ("zero fees, zero tracking, zero dependencies")
- [ ] Ensure robots.txt allows GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot
- [ ] Consider adding `llms.txt` for AI crawler guidance
- [ ] Keep factual claims verifiable (link to on-chain data, source code)

## Risk Considerations

- **Regulatory:** Crypto SEO may face advertising restrictions in some jurisdictions. Avoid making financial promises.
- **YMYL:** Cryptocurrency content falls under Google's "Your Money or Your Life" category — E-E-A-T signals are critical.
- **Competition:** Major swap aggregators have large SEO budgets. Focus on the zero-fee niche rather than competing head-on.
- **Content freshness:** Token lists and supported chains change — ensure programmatic pages stay current.
